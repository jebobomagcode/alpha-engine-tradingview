## 1. Product and release scope

Open a standard 5m chart for a qualified Binance perpetual. The indicator identifies the exact feed, reads that contract’s confirmed 4H, 1H and 15m evidence, and updates its analysis at the close of each eligible 5m bar. It shows what LONG and SHORT continuation would need next, which conditions were observed, whether a setup qualified, and whether an informational price geometry can be computed. The chart explains missing or conflicting evidence and retains dated events when the current state changes.

| Scope | Candidate requirement |
| --- | --- |
| Destination | Support all positively qualified Binance USDⓈ-M perpetuals quoted and settled in a verified USD stablecoin. USDT and USDC are the first quote and settlement currencies. Admit another stablecoin or a contract with different quote and settlement currencies only after verifying both units and the product rules. |
| First private successor | Qualify one USDT and one USDC contract with different tick sizes on a standard time-based 5m host. Select exact instruments by metadata, trade-feed identity, continuity, usable four-layer history and native acceptance. The exact launch symbols remain to be qualified. |
| Next market increment | Prioritize exact Binance USDⓈ-M **XAUUSDT**. Qualify its raw product variant, feed mapping, session schedule and time boundaries, units and tick, warmup and native cases. Keep it distinct from XAUTUSDT, PAXGUSDT and any Spot, mark or index feed. |
| Initial profile | One mirrored LONG/SHORT continuation profile. Range, reversal, countertrend and conditional breakout/retest receive named research coverage and may have future profiles. |
| Operating boundary | Single-chart, private research indicator. No order placement, live trade recommendation, sizing, fill inference or profitability claim. Scanner, alerts, automation, higher chart hosts and ChatGPT exchange of evidence are separate optional work. |

## 2. Contract admission and evidence

Keep a versioned manifest and an immutable evidence receipt for each admitted feed. Separate **product in scope**, **exact chart mapped**, **native accepted**, and **data ready at cutoff**. Admission requires the first three. Decisions require the fourth for every dependency they use. A positively identified but unaccepted feed may explain the restriction and display no setup or risk drawings.

- **Product identity.** Record Binance USDⓈ-M market segment, exchange symbol, pair, raw contract type and status, normalized perpetual family, base, quote, margin and verified settlement units, product-specific multiplier or unit convention, and metadata effective/observed times and validity policy. Preserve the raw `TRADIFI_PERPETUAL` type when present in evidence and independently verify its perpetual mapping. Preserve explicit unknowns. A blank base in a provider response requires separate positive identity evidence. Use `PRICE_FILTER.tickSize` and relevant exchange price rules. Retain size and notional filters as metadata when available. Price display precision, suffix and chart tick alone cannot prove identity.
- **Chart identity.** Bind the exact TradingView exchange ticker ID and price series to Binance trade-price OHLC. Confirm standard 5m time-based OHLC and test the ordinary candlestick view. Reject synthetic chart construction, wrong timeframe, derivatives from another exchange, Spot and mark/index substitutions. Record known session rules and expected missing intervals. Compare TradingView tick and timestamps to the manifest. Use an externally verified manifest compiled or otherwise versioned with the build. Pine cannot authenticate current settlement or discover the current eligible universe from Binance REST. If metadata is explicitly stale, changed or unverified, show that status and withhold qualification. Pin the reviewed symbol and metadata versions for historical comparisons.
- **Evidence record.** Store provider, exact endpoint and request scope, retrieval time, raw hash, normalization version, instrument and price basis, market metadata ID, UTC interval open and exclusive close, finality, OHLC and volume units when used, and raw versus normalized timestamp fields. For a documented inclusive millisecond endpoint close, normalize to the next boundary and retain the original field. PHT is display-only. Compare prices on the exchange grid and timestamps exactly. Exclude developing bars. Quarantine conflicting duplicates and out-of-order records. Exact duplicates can be deduplicated with a diagnostic. Missing expected bars break required adjacency and trigger dependency-level insufficient evidence. Expected closures in a qualified session are a separate case. Never synthesize a missing OHLC bar.
- **Availability and revisions.** Record modeled availability, runtime first-seen evidence when observable, and verified receipt time when separately captured. Historical OHLC supplies no authenticated native receipt clock or physical touch order. Preserve provider corrections as new evidence versions. Later arrivals can trigger current reassessment and a new audit record. Keep any original as-issued trace attributable to its original input version. A later reload can reconstruct a different trace from changed inputs and must be labeled accordingly.

## 3. Four timeframes and decision clocks

| Layer | Role in the first profile | Behavior when different or unavailable |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 4H | Confirmed directional structure. Up authorizes LONG continuation evaluation and down authorizes SHORT continuation evaluation. | Mixed/neutral means wait for a regime. Missing or stale means insufficient. A valid reaction in the opposing direction can still be reported as an observation. |
| 1H | Confirmed intermediate structure and supporting, neutral or opposing context relative to each side. | The candidate baseline requires enough data to evaluate 1H. Opposing structure remains visible and the baseline can still qualify. Compare a version with a hard 1H alignment gate on identical inputs. |
| 15m | Primary pre-known S/R zone for each side and eligible structural references for optional targets. | A valid pullback can oppose the 4H direction. No zone means wait. Unknown boundary, stale packet or insufficient structure history withholds dependent evaluation. |
| 5m | Completed-bar overlap, directional reaction, episode transitions and output cutoff. | A developing candle can change chart price only. It cannot create a final contact, reaction, qualification, invalidation or risk guide. Missing 5m evidence cannot establish a reaction or reset. |

For a host interval `[o, T)`, lock the eligible HTF packet set at modeled open `o`. At final 5m close `T`, evaluate the closed host bar against that opening snapshot. Carry source origin, source confirmation, arming time where relevant, modeled known-at, actual observed first-seen where available, host open, decision cutoff, and display/event time. Compute each layer in its own timeframe. Opening snapshot values do not update within that host interval. A newly confirmed source object first affects a decision on a host bar whose opening snapshot includes it.

Example on aligned UTC trade feeds. A zone confirmed at 10:15 cannot govern the 5m bar ending at 10:15. With the confirmed packet in the 10:15 opening snapshot, the first applicable decision is 10:20. If a realtime packet is observed only after the opening snapshot, do not insert it into that interval’s decision. Re-evaluate at a later opening with the packet present and still fresh. Historical Pine normally models the packet at the provider’s historical mapping boundary. Report reconstruction and observed runtime behavior separately, including boundary races and reload differences.

Use one fixed source-timeframe packet per HTF from the same manifest-bound ticker. Use previous-HTF-bar offsets for all dynamic fields with `lookahead_on` on strictly higher requests. Gate committed host state at closed 5m execution. Deduplicate updates by source interval and version. Compare held packet timestamps to the expected last completed interval for the qualified session. Fix warmup bounds, seed policies and tie handling with the rule version. For identical inputs and versions, future appends must leave every earlier event and geometry record unchanged. Native first-update capture and historical/reload tests must show whether Pine reproduces the intended mapping at 15m, 1H and 4H boundaries. A realtime opening latch must survive Pine rollback within the host bar. If a script is attached mid-bar and cannot establish the opening packet set, withhold that live decision until the next eligible opening. Do not silently fall back to a closing snapshot.

## 4. Methodology and experimental defaults

The [source manifest](../SOURCE_MANIFEST.md) identifies the sole Coach-authored PDF. Its SHA-256 was verified against the project copy. Lesson and Q&A links below are PCSS records. Provenance labels are `COACH_EXPLICIT` for direct PDF evidence, `INTERPRETATION` for PCSS formalization, `INDEPENDENT_EXTENSION / EXPERIMENTAL` for the proposed detectors, and `VALIDATED` or `OPEN_UNRESOLVED` for the associated evidence status. Display provenance by rule or overlay. A paper or native acceptance result establishes only the tested behavior.

| Mechanism | Proposed deterministic baseline | Comparator or coverage |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Structural swings and direction | Use strict 2-left/2-right pivots on consecutive finalized bars in each applicable source timeframe. Confirm at the second right close. Equal highs/lows in the pivot window produce no strict pivot on that side. An outside candle qualifying as both sides cannot determine order. For ordered direction, reset the sequence on a dual pivot; on a same-side pivot, restart from the newer pivot. Require four consecutive alternating pivots for classification. Up needs the last high and low both higher than their prior counterpart, down needs both lower, and other complete sequences are mixed. Distinguish warming/incomplete from mixed. | Strict 1/1 and 3/3, and an extreme-retaining same-side policy. None claims to recover all discretionary swings. |
| Reference breaks | Retain the project’s provisional R1-A timing: if the immediately prior eligible final close available at arming is already beyond the threshold, block that reference; if it is missing, eligibility is unknown. Require the armed state at the crossing interval’s open and a fresh final close from the non-break side to strictly beyond. Consume once and retire on replacement, expiry or evidence reset. BOS follows established structure; a break against it is possible CHoCH. Neither alone reclassifies confirmed structure. | R1-B requires a distinct closed reset, known by the crossing open, followed by a fresh cross. Compare delay, blocked and recovered cases with identical references. |
| 15m S/R | Use a band one price tick either side of an eligible confirmed 15m pivot low for LONG support or high for SHORT resistance. Exclude a dual pivot from new zones in this baseline. One-tick width is a sensitivity baseline. Enforce valid price bounds, causal arming and a defined far edge. For this zone eligibility check, use the arming snapshot against the far edge and the same missing-predecessor principle as R1-A. A zone and its reference break state remain separate objects. | Compare a width normalized by 15m volatility or percentage and review event-rate differences across tick sizes. Do not transfer a single economic interpretation of one tick across products. |
| 5m confirmation | Use a directional real-body engulf of the immediately adjacent final bar. LONG requires a bearish prior body, bullish current body, current open at or below prior close, and current close strictly above prior open. SHORT mirrors the inequalities and colors. Allowing equality at the opening edge avoids demanding a price gap between adjacent bars. Dojis, equality at the closing edge and missing predecessors fail this baseline pattern. Use R2-B for same-bar geometric overlap plus reaction, or a qualifying later closed reaction in the same episode. | R2-A needs an earlier distinct closed overlap. Strict requires independently established touch receipt before reaction onset and remains unknown when that order is unavailable. Test strict opening-edge engulfing and optional 5m BOS and reclaim filters separately. |
| Context overlays | Keep OB, supply/demand, FVG, Fibonacci, EMA and RSI contextual when a versioned detector and the relevant warmup exist. Preserve separate sources and lifecycles. No aggregate confidence score or cumulative mandatory indicator gate. | Compare marginal information, delay and missed valid cases on fixed inputs before promoting any context feature into a hard rule. |

## 5. Setup and contact lifecycle

The state record carries feed and evidence identity, four packet identities, build/manifest/profile versions, side, zone and reference IDs, episode ID, cutoff, observation status, qualification, current state, blocker and geometry status. Key the zone by its source interval, pivot origin/confirmation, side and rule identity, with a revision for material boundary or evidence changes. Key the setup by feed, profile/rule version, side and pinned zone version. Key contact episodes by setup and first accepted contact cutoff. A new build alone does not mint a fresh historical event under identical rule/evidence identities. Store as-issued provenance separately. A primary zone is chosen before the bar from ready candidates, using newest confirmation, then newest origin, then lexicographically smallest stable ID. Track invalidation and expiry for every retained zone even while unselected. A zone with incomplete intervening evidence must be re-established by a fresh eligible reference, so an old band cannot first appear valid simply because it was not displayed. Evaluate both sides separately. Keep the selected zone pinned from accepted contact until terminal resolution. Replacement before contact or after termination is prospective.

| Condition at an eligible cutoff | Transition and published evidence |
| --- | --- |
| Dependencies known, no qualifying visit yet | `WAIT` with a reason such as mixed regime, no eligible zone or waiting for approach/contact. Price must approach support from strictly above the near edge or resistance from strictly below it using the immediately previous adjacent final 5m close. |
| Eligible opening snapshot, closed range overlaps the band | `CONTACT`. Record geometric overlap only. Keep overlapping bars in the same episode. No overlap and a close on the wrong side can invalidate without establishing contact. |
| Directional closed reaction under the selected timing rule | Record a `REACTION` observation. If 4H, 1H readiness, zone, known invalidation boundary, episode and all timing gates pass, record one `SETUP QUALIFIED` event for that episode. An opposite 4H reaction or an out-of-profile pattern remains an observation with a reason. |
| Defined boundary breached | For a selected zone, LONG closes strictly below the support far edge, SHORT closes strictly above the resistance far edge. Apply the boundary even before first contact. Equality is not a breach. Record `INVALIDATED`; retain previous events and suppress current qualification/geometry. A same-close breach outranks contact and reaction qualification even if overlap or a pattern was observed. |
| No reaction by the deadline, or active window/zone lifetime ends | `EXPIRED`. On an exclusive deadline the decision at the boundary is ineligible. Expiry and invalidation can be recorded together when both are evidenced, with invalidation the primary terminal reason. An expired ID cannot reactivate. |
| A wholly approach-side closed bar ends an existing visit | Use a bar with low strictly above support or high strictly below resistance as the separator. It may contain the first eligible **later-bar** reaction before recording `REASSESS` for the episode at that same cutoff. Show the dated qualification and current `REASSESS` separately. Current geometry is cleared. Re-entry needs a new eligible overlap after the separator is known. |
| Product or required packet is missing, revised materially or loses its context | Show `INSUFFICIENT` with layer and timestamp, or `REASSESS` for a documented material change. The first profile ends the affected episode on a missing interval or loss of required context. An episode observed against the 4H regime cannot inherit qualification when 4H later aligns. Start a fresh aligned visit. Retain independently valid observations. Rebuild prerequisites and require a fresh eligible approach/contact. Never bridge a gap or revive a terminal episode. |

For a bounded experimental starting version, define zone expiry at first eligible host open + 60 minutes, reaction eligibility at the contact cutoff and the next two consecutive 5m closes, and current qualification until qualification cutoff + 15 minutes exclusive. For example, contact at 10:15 permits 10:15, 10:20 and 10:25 reactions before the 10:30 exclusive deadline, unless an earlier terminal condition applies. The earliest applicable zone or episode terminal condition wins. These are reproducible comparison windows of four 15m periods and one 15m period, not a Coach rule, market-optimized setting or preferred holding time. For reset and expiry compare against a no-carry post-contact variant and a longer still-bounded variant on the same fixtures. Session interruptions or data gaps cannot extend or revive an episode. Zone replacement, deadline, boundary breach, data failure, context loss and departure each have separate reasons. Process valid terminal evidence before new qualification, except the documented departure case, which can emit a historical event and terminal current state at the same cutoff.

## 6. Geometry and presentation

**Qualification requires a defensible invalidation boundary. A valid nominal stop or target is a separate question.** Pass a currently qualified episode and its frozen evidence to geometry. Report `GEOMETRY VALID` or `GEOMETRY UNAVAILABLE` with the missing or contradictory field. Examples include no eligible prior target, stop outside venue price bounds, prices off grid, zero price distance, wrong-side target, or a reference consumed at the cutoff. If the invalidation boundary itself is unknown, retain the observed reaction and withhold setup qualification.

For a reproducible first geometry version, use the 5m confirmation close as the *nominal* entry, one tick beyond the known band far edge as the candidate stop, and the nearest eligible unconsumed opposing 15m structural level in the profitable direction as the candidate target. The target must have been in the opening snapshot and must still be eligible at the decision. Preserve prices as validated tick-grid values and withhold rather than silently round a contradictory input. An equal target distance tie uses the zone ranking tie-break. With side sign `s = +1` for LONG and `s = -1` for SHORT, require `risk = s × (E − SL) > 0` and `reward = s × (TP − E) > 0`. Report nominal `R = reward / risk` using the displayed values in verified quote-price units. Never infer a fill or compute account risk from price distance alone. Record geometry at qualification if the setup is still current after terminal checks. A historical qualification on the departure bar produces no current risk guide. Clear the current guide if the episode ends or any required geometry reference loses eligibility. No silent retargeting of that event. Structural target versus fixed-R, alternate stops and cost-aware geometry remain comparisons. Execution readiness is **not assessed** at either geometry status. [M06 risk contract](../methodology/09-m06-risk-and-execution-uncertainty.md).

**Display.** Provide a compact dashboard with exact chart identity, build/profile, four source-close rows and independently readable LONG/SHORT status, blocker and geometry. A diagnostic mode exposes evidence provenance, packet times, modeled versus observed status, and IDs. Start with active S/R bands, bounded structural labels and BOS/possible CHoCH, contact, reaction and invalidation markers, and optional nominal geometry. Offer existing OB, supply/demand, FVG, Fibonacci and EMA/RSI context only for the overlays whose 5m definitions and native parity are preserved, labeled with timeframe and policy. Any promoted 15m variant needs its own evidence and tests. Use distinct presentation for observed, experimental, historical and active states. Start qualifying drawings at their decision cutoff. An optional origin view can connect to the source interval with an explicit retrospective label. Remove active styling on terminal events and retain a capped event history. Show one current zone per side and retire stale objects deterministically. Unsupported charts get a compact diagnostic and no active setup drawings.

**Native design limits.** One instrument, three fixed HTF requests, bounded histories and drawing budgets, no intrabar reconstruction on higher host charts. Version the budgets and test exhaustion, replacement and ordinary/constrained viewports. The chart cannot reliably serve as a durable as-issued evidence log. Use separately captured native traces for live versus reload claims. [TradingView Pine limits](https://www.tradingview.com/pine-script-docs/writing/limitations/).
