### Independent numerical check — NOT in Coach source

For a SHORT, price risk S−E, gross price reward E−T, with S>E>T.
- Risk = 79,918−79,419 = **499** USD per BTC.
- Reward = 79,419−78,423 = **996** USD per BTC.
- Gross RR = 996 / 499 = **1.995991984:1**, approximately **1:2**, so student is correct at ordinary rounding.
- Stop distance = 499/79,419 ≈ **0.6283%** of entry, reward distance = 996/79,419 ≈ **1.2541%**.

Price-distance percentage is NOT account risk; net after-cost RR and trade expectancy cannot be inferred without fees, size and fill policy.
