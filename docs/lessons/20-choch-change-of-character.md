## 1. Original definition and classification

**COACH EXPLICIT:** “CHoCH (Change of Character) - possible reversal or shift ng trend”. Unlike a BOS in the direction of the previously established trend, a CHoCH concerns a break **against that previous structure**.

- From an established **downtrend**, price breaks a **previous Lower High (LH)**: Coach calls it CHoCH, with a *chance* of an upward reversal.
- From an established **uptrend**, price breaks a **previous Higher Low (HL)**: Coach identifies a *possible* downward move.

**Critical distinction:** A CHoCH is an *early structural warning* or candidate, **not** a guarantee of subsequent reversal and not a standalone order. Coach gives no numerical win probability, point target, timeframe or strict candle-break test on this page.

### Exact source diagram F1 — Market Structure

Upper-left schematic begins with rising price legs and **three green bullish BOS labels**; a downward crossing of a prior swing low is marked **red CHoCH**, after which **three red bearish BOS labels** accompany the falling market; on the right an upward break is marked **green CHoCH**. This explicitly illustrates why the same kind of level break can be BOS or CHoCH depending on the preceding trend.

**Independent interpretation:** Prior swing classification must exist *before* interpreting a later break. A break against an UP structure may be CHoCH DOWN, whereas a break below prior lows when structure is already DOWN may be a continuation BOS DOWN. Same for UP.

## 3. Independent numerical teaching examples — NOT Coach source

**Bearish CHoCH candidate:** Suppose known higher-low support is at 103 USDT in a prior uptrend and a later completed close is 102. Under one *independent close-break policy*, 102<103 signals a possible bearish structural shift, not a confirmed sustained DOWN trend.

**Bullish CHoCH candidate:** Suppose the relevant prior Lower High is 104 USDT in a downtrend and a later completed close is 106. Under that same *hypothetical close-break policy*, 106>104 is a possible bullish structural shift.

**Crucial missing information:** The source does not say the close is compulsory; the same candle might have pierced the level with a wick and closed back below it. No minimum price buffer, session restriction, gap handling, equal-high/low policy, volume, displacement, post-break retest, or subsequent structural event is supplied.

## 4. Source gap audit — do not invent a deterministic Coach rule

**Which prior swing?** “Previous LH/HL” is not a globally unique value on a chart with internal and external structure, minor pivots and several timeframes. A formal method must explicitly select the last *eligible confirmed* lower high/higher low or “protected” swing. The concept of a protected swing is an INDEPENDENT formalization term here, not Coach's own explicit p25 definition.

**When known?** A swing marker appearing at an earlier bar is often recognizable only after later bars. A right-confirmed pivot can become known several candles after the visible turning point, creating lookahead if signals are backdated to the turning point. The source does not define pivot latency.

**What constitutes “break”?** Wick/high-low penetration versus completed close beyond the level; strict inequality and tick buffer; ties/equal swings; candle body/wick and break confirmation are unresolved. Detecting an instantaneous high above LH is different from declaring a confirmed bullish CHoCH at the candle close.

**What next?** CHoCH does not automatically specify an order, stop or TP, nor prove bullish or bearish regime conversion. Require objective evidence if labeling *confirmed sustained new trend*, and do not silently call the CHoCH itself both “possible reversal” and “confirmed reversal”.

**Multiple timescales:** The p25 illustrations give no exact timeframe or crypto exchange. A 5m CHoCH could be a transient retracement while 4H remains UP/DOWN. Preserve event timeframe and higher-timeframe context separately.

**Market universality:** Source does not prove CHoCH is profitable on BTC, ETH, XAUUSDT, XAUTUSDT or PAXGUSDT, nor that they share price/volume histories.

## 5. Independent research and proposed state model (NOT implemented)

Future research could represent:
- prior structural regime: UP, DOWN, MIXED or UNKNOWN, with identified swing level and known-at time;
- candidate break: WICK_BREACH, CLOSED_BREAK or NOT_BROKEN, each with its own definition;
- CHOCH_BULL_CANDIDATE / CHOCH_BEAR_CANDIDATE only if prior regime and eligible opposite-side break coexist;
- separate later BOS or confirmation as potential stronger reversal-context evidence, **not an automatically Coach-prescribed gate**;
- WAIT/INSUFFICIENT for pending pivot confirmation, unclosed break, unknown timeframe or data integrity issues;
- actual trade eligibility and stop/TP assessed independently, not by drawing a green/red box.
