## Page34: Source timeframes and fundamentals

COACH EXPLICIT: Philippine stocks prefer **Weekly, Daily, 4H** analysis, because price is strongly influenced by **earnings, quarterly reports, BSP interest rates, inflation, economic growth** and other factors. Technical charting is used later to help choose entry. Independent context: fundamentals affect expected future profits and valuation, but there is no deterministic earnings-to-candle direction rule.

Independent exchange distinction: PSE normal sessions run weekdays and include intraday breaks/overnight gaps. [PSE investor trading hours](https://www.pse.com.ph/investing-at-pse/). A PSE 4H candle cannot automatically be treated as informationally identical to a continuous crypto 4H candle. Market sessions, bar-close timing, corporate-action-adjusted prices and liquidity must be evaluated per feed.
