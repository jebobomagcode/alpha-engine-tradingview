## D. Invariants to be converted into executable assertions only when separately authorized

1. **As-of snapshot:** every supporting HTF/LTF bar, swing, zone and reaction has available_at <= decision_at.
2. **No backdating:** any actionable classification first issued at decision time must use evidence available by that decision and satisfy the applicable arming and freeze rules. A pivot, candle or zone may have an earlier historical origin coordinate. Test the origin coordinate, first eligible boundary, first publication and archived as-of output separately. A newly annotated older origin cannot change an already published historical decision. This wording supersedes the earlier ambiguous use of “event time” (12 November, E2).
3. **No mutable past:** processing a new 4H bar may revise current interpretation, not the prior recorded snapshot from a 5m bar.
4. **Stable symbol:** event must share an exchange-qualified product/feed identity; XAUUSDT perpetual != XAUTUSDT spot != PAXGUSDT.
5. **Wait means wait:** in Lesson08 template absent eligible contact => no entry candidate; absent closed LTF reaction => WAIT.
6. **Contrary LTF:** opposite LTF structure does not instantly flip 4H regime.
7. **CHoCH scope:** LTF possible change never equals HTF confirmed reversal without independent HTF evidence.
8. **Missing != false:** unavailable required RSI/HTF is INSUFFICIENT, not bearish; optional absent RSI does not automatically veto.
9. **Conflict preservation:** simultaneous opposing conditional candidates retain both individual reasons; no implied simultaneous actual positions.
10. **Indicator context distinct:** duplicated transformations such as same close above EMA and positive one-bar EMA slope are not two independent score votes.
11. **Invalidation priority:** candidate invalidation stops its current entry readiness; previous historical state is preserved.
12. **Zero actual PnL until execution simulation:** no source chart or hypothetical RR produces a verified return.
13. **Timeframe scheduling:** a newly ending 4H bar cannot govern an earlier 5m candle under MTF-T0; MTF-T1 only after its verified availability.
14. **Intra-bar order unknown:** a candle OHLC range does not prove order placement/fill price or which touched level first.
15. **A known unresolved ambiguity remains labelled:** do not silently invent exact candle ratio, range breakout method, session schedule or OB color criterion.

## J. Causal input and output contract

- **Manifest.** Record product, price basis, evidence version and hashes, UTC interval and bar IDs, initial history bound, policy/version, context and support zone versions, direction, invalidation, expiry and optional risk input versions. For context and zone, record source reference, effective time and availability provenance. Explicitly distinguish independently logged issuance from a modeled historical annotation. A chart input edited later cannot prove when an analyst first knew a zone. A reconstructed case remains marked as such.
- **Opening freeze.** At the open of the 5m candle, select the latest eligible completed 1H evidence under T0 and context/zone versions already known. In the modeled bar clock only, a final 1H row may become available at the first 5m opening of the new hour. If the expected 1H identity is missing or is delayed beyond the opening in a receipt-based case, record the missing or late premise for that candle. Historical kline endpoints carry no observation of native arrival order. Example: the 09:55–10:00 5m bar uses the hourly reference ending 09:00. The 10:00–10:05 bar may use the hourly reference ending 10:00, with its first close-based decision at 10:05 under modeled availability. A new hourly reference at 10:00 cannot alter the first case.
- **Closing decision.** Evaluate from completed and available 5m inputs at the permitted cutoff, checking known invalidation and exclusive expiry before fresh eligibility at that cutoff. A long close strictly below the support floor invalidates the profile. A newly available material context change may suspend current eligibility for REASSESS without replacing the opening premise. Keep the formed bar, modeled boundary, independent observation if present, first eligibility, and display coordinate as separate fields. A closed geometric overlap is inclusive: the high reaches at least the zone floor and the low reaches at most the zone ceiling. This establishes a range intersection. Exact touch price, path and order activation remain unknown unless separately evidenced. For delayed evidence, qualify only at the first allowed decision containing the required prefix, subject to the frozen opening premises and active lifecycle.
- **Identity and state.** Assign setup ID before contact from the instrument/profile and a stable declared setup/version ID. Persist its context/zone version and activation provenance. Assign the contact ID from setup ID plus first eligible touch-bar identity and keep reaction IDs separate. Display reason and first qualifying cutoff by policy, together with current WAIT_ZONE, WAIT_CONFIRMATION, insufficient evidence, RISK_INCOMPLETE, INVALIDATED, EXPIRED or REASSESS as applicable. Preserve earlier as-issued records after a correction, invalidate/expire before new qualifying decisions when known, and suspend current candidacy during unresolved reassessment. A declared replacement needs a new version. Missing predecessors or stale required HTF evidence keep the appropriate evidence gate unresolved.
- **As-of reproducibility.** Hold start, inputs, versions, exact normalized records and availability assumptions fixed. Appending later evidence must leave every already archived prefix and its output reproducible. A revision can change the current interpretation without editing its predecessor. Preserve decision and observation evidence separately from chart markers that may be drawn at earlier origin coordinates. Record adverse assumptions separately from observed price data.
