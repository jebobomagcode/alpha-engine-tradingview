## Candidate formation and ordered structure

| Candidate | Explicit research definition | Discriminating weakness |
| --- | --- | --- |
| S0, manual comparator | At each frozen prefix a reviewer records accepted swing side, origin bar, exact tick level, scale tag, role, optional protected reference, regime, evidence IDs, declaration time and ambiguity reason. Freeze the record on submission. A later correction is a new revision with later availability. Allow abstention. | Manual selection resembles the source process but can have hindsight, inconsistent scale and high reviewer disagreement. A retrospectively authored input has a modeled known-at, not witnessed historical availability. |
| S1, strict extrema | On a valid contiguous final window, high must exceed every other high, and low must be below every other low. Left/right `2/2` is a comparison seed matching the accepted probe, with `1/1` and `3/3` as sensitivity checks. No amplitude or separation cutoffs selected. Pivot availability is the maximum availability of the window. | Window size controls local neighborhood and delay, not market significance. A one-tick ripple can qualify, a tied structural turn can disappear, and nested swings need a scale convention. |
| S2, directional change | Separate challenger on **final closes**. Declare an integer reversal distance `d > 0` and initial phase before evaluation. In an upswing, retain the greatest observed close and confirm it as a turning candidate at the first close at or below that extreme minus `d`. Mirror in a downswing. Retain earliest origin on equal closes and store later confirmation. Start a new epoch after unusable evidence. | Measures price reversal amplitude instead of neighborhood. Can ignore wick extremes used by an analyst. Confirmation delay varies. Any OHLC variant needs an explicit treatment of two-sided crossings. The initial phase and `d` are research inputs, with no preferred value. |
| S3, rolling comparator | Compute previous `N` contiguous, valid, available final bars' max high/min low, excluding the current bar. Compare current close strictly above or below. Record only a rolling extreme crossing. | Break can occur with zero confirmed swing pairs or known regime. Useful as a negative control for structural specificity. No selected `N`. |

**Ordered S1 baseline, experimental.** Keep raw observations immutable. A strict tie on one side cancels that side only. A dual extreme preserves both observations and breaks the ordered segment. Gaps, malformed or incomplete bars, unresolved simultaneous evidence and out-of-order origins split or suspend the chain. Never insert a late observation into an old chain. If received together, distinct known origin times can be ordered as a batch if their continuous windows are verified and both origins extend the current chain. A dual origin remains unordered. An overlapping window may still confirm local extrema when its own bars are sound. Do not use that fact to bridge a discontinuous ordered segment.

**Same-side policies.** Quarantine the ambiguous run for classification: on a successive same-side candidate, begin a fresh segment with that latest candidate, keeping both raw records. As a challenger, retain the more extreme of consecutive same-side candidates as a provisional end of the current leg, preferring the earlier origin on equal prices. Neither may replace an already frozen reference retrospectively. A previously issued event remains in the ledger even when a later prefix gives a different current regime. Never splice a new candidate before a previously released origin. Low-side rules mirror high-side rules.

**Regime.** In the chosen continuous, ordered, alternating segment, require at least two highs and two lows at the same declared scale. Compare the last two of each side at that prefix regardless of the starting side of the last four pivots. Both strictly rising establishes UP; both strictly falling establishes DOWN; ties, mixed signs or insufficient order yield UNKNOWN. This is an independent rule for a candidate snapshot. Use the most recent qualified structural HH/HL or LH/LL only when explicitly assigned as a reference in that snapshot, with coherent high/low geometry, rather than letting an arriving pivot re-label an older break. Structural regime and break evaluation are distinct. For a possible shift, retain the prior regime as event context, put current structural evaluation in `POSSIBLE_SHIFT` and retire old-regime live references. Opposite context requires a newly accepted, versioned regime declaration or a fresh ordered proof under its own experiment. A single shift, one new swing or a later reinterpretation does not suffice.

## Causal reference, arming and break contract

| Record | Clock and identity |
| --- | --- |
| Final bar | Venue, product, instrument, price basis, interval, standard OHLC, UTC opening and nominal closing boundary, receipt or modeled availability, evidence version and finality. Qualified bars are contiguous, unique, positive, geometrically valid integer ticks. A missing or invalid interval is not a flat candle. |
| Pivot | Origin bar and side, level, scale, qualifying window and rule version. Record the rightmost nominal closing boundary separately from evidence availability and actual first confirmation. Keep missing or pending evidence explicit. |
| Regime and role | Version, regime scale/timeframe, ordered evidence IDs, known-at for each premise and the role designation. Manual declarations require matching market, timeframe and exact as-of provenance. Evidence must have been available by the designated cutoff. |
| Reference | Freeze semantic anchor (market/TF/price basis, scale, origin, side and level), regime/role, declaration revision, comparison policy and buffer. A new role or replay key does not erase consumption of the same underlying anchor within the same policy stream. A genuinely independent reference requires affirmative new evidence. Keep alternative policy ledgers separate. |
| Break and publication | Record predecessor and crossing bar intervals, availability, decision timestamp, reference lifecycle, and first publication. Use the pivot origin only as explanatory metadata. Corrections arrive as new evidence versions and can drive reassessment, without rewriting the immutable as-of ledger. |

**Equal wall-clock boundary.** In the zero-latency historical chart model, order phases at UTC boundary `T`: (1) admit closing evidence of the bar ending at `T`; (2) evaluate that bar against the reference snapshot frozen at its own open; (3) validate and freeze arming/reset/retirement state for the bar starting at `T`; (4) record its opening snapshot. Step 3 logically precedes step 4 at the same nominal time. A final bar that establishes a new premise cannot qualify its own crossing. A declaration or bar arriving after the opening snapshot first contributes to a later modeled open. Preserve the nominal closing boundary, evidence availability and decision/publication separately. Missing native receipt order yields UNKNOWN for actual open-time eligibility, even if a modeled chart sequence is reproducible.

Use the immediately preceding final bar available by the opening snapshot and the current available final bar. At arming, check the immediately preceding bar as of that boundary, even if the reference is evaluated later. Earlier valid bars cannot substitute for a missing adjacent predecessor. Pending/unknown inputs withhold classification. A late final that arrives after a modeled evaluation does not retroactively fill the absent decision. The policy can resume at a new complete boundary subject to the reference's separate validity rules. Model retirement before subsequent opening snapshots; when later contrary evidence arrives, preserve earlier decisions. Never imply that publication-time awareness was possible at an earlier bar.

Let `L` be integer reference ticks and `b >= 0` a frozen integer buffer. For a high use threshold `L+b`, and require `prior close <= threshold < current close`. For a low use `L-b`, and require `current close < threshold <= prior close`. Reject nonpositive thresholds and incoherent levels. Equality is unbroken. With a one-tick buffer and a strict comparison, the earliest high-side qualifying close is `L+2` ticks, and low side is symmetric. “At least one tick beyond the level” on this grid is the zero-buffer strict test. No preferred buffer.

| Arming policy | Same evidence, different eligibility |
| --- | --- |
| R1-A, latest close | If the adjacent at-arming close is on the unbroken side, allow a later fresh crossing. If already beyond, block that anchor for this comparison. Missing adjacent close is INSUFFICIENT. Preserve the at-arming state separately from any earlier excursion. |
| R1-B, reset/re-cross | If already beyond, record `RESET_PENDING`. First later available unbroken close resets eligibility at its next admissible opening phase. A different bar must then cross freshly. A consumed anchor cannot reset. Without independent age evidence this can recycle stale unconsumed levels, so keep replacement/expiry open. |
| R1-C, manual reference provenance | Manual structural selection specifies a qualified anchor, role, evidence and as-of availability. Evaluate the same anchor with A, B or history veto explicitly selected. Manual sourcing itself cannot determine reset or consumption policy or legitimize an old crossing. |
| Complete-history veto | Use A and require every expected qualified final close with nominal closing boundary in `(pivot nominal confirmation boundary, arming boundary]`, available by arming. Any beyond close vetoes even if it reverted. Incomplete history is INSUFFICIENT for this comparison. Include at-arming close, and validate the adjacent predecessor independently if the history interval is empty. Earlier origin-window prices are pivot evidence, not pre-arm excursions for this hypothesis. |

Only a fresh eligible close crossing is consumed in this baseline. A separate wick diagnostic uses current `high > high threshold` or `low < low threshold` on a bar with an eligible prior unbroken close. It can coexist with a closed crossing or occur without one and cannot establish intrabar order or a fill. An outside bar can test both levels, while one close cannot strictly cross both disjoint thresholds of a coherent pair. Map UP/HH and DOWN/LL to continuation candidates and DOWN/LH and UP/HL to possible-shift candidates. Even a correctly classified event supplies no support/resistance contact, closed Lesson 08 reaction, entry, or execution evidence.

## Verification and design decisions

- **Necessary:** order only evidence that can be ordered; reject impossible strict-pivot windows; freeze prior decisions; use matching price units and provenance; preserve possible shift separately from reversal and from Lesson 08.
- **Experimental:** quarantine and chain resets, compression, exact window/scale, close/wick, buffer, history window, R1-B reset, post-shift handover and time-based expiry. Reversal criteria include timestamped source-compatible swings missed at fixed scale, systematic fresh events after legitimate returns that veto suppresses, or late/stale references that latest-close admits.
- **Accepted evidence boundary:** [Phase 3.3 probe](../implementation/phase3-3-m01-pivot-probe.md) checks a strict-window observation, [Phase 3.4](../implementation/phase3-4-lesson08-m01-isolation.md) checks Lesson 08 isolation and [Phase 3.5](../implementation/phase3-5-private-persistence-acceptance.md) checks bounded private persistence. None validates a structural sequence, role classifier, or native receipt ordering.
- **Future empirical gate:** lock symbol, scale instructions and as-of prefixes, hide future bars, predeclare adjudication and match tolerances, and seek two independent reviewers with timestamped annotations and abstentions. Stratify by trend/range, nested turns, ties, duals, gaps and late context. Compare inter-reviewer agreement, reference match and disagreement by reason, false promotion, coverage and delay on held-out prefixes. Establish acceptable disagreement before sampling. Change scale, amplitude or minimum separation only for documented structural agreement or observability, never by trading returns.
