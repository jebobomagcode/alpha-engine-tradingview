## Case conventions

One fictional qualified instrument, standard bars, one clock and a fixed policy per run. Times below share a reference clock for comparison. A bar denoted [10:00, 10:05) closes nominally at 10:05. When an input is said to be included by another boundary at the same nominal time, inclusion is stipulated by a witnessed or modeled tie order. When a tie order is unknown, the strict observed-sequence check cannot pass. Timestamps with seconds are used to expose receipt differences.

Prices use (O,H,L,C) order. The default continuation setup has a finalized 1H UP annotation and active support [99,100] included by 10:00. Closed-bar overlap and strict real-body engulfing are as defined in the companion spec. For a bullish engulf, a red previous candle’s body lies strictly inside the green current candle’s body. Reaction completion is evaluated after all required final inputs are included. Unless a case states otherwise, decisions are evaluated when the needed evidence is available, with adequate remaining lifetime and unchanged context. Full risk inputs are absent.

**Reaction policies in the tables.** Same = R2-B. Later = R2-A by later bar order. Known = R2-A with touch included by reaction onset. “Reaction qualified” refers to the descriptive reaction gate. M05 risk status is RISK_INCOMPLETE in these continuation cases.
