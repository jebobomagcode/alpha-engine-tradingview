# M06 conceptual paper cases and manual adjudication

**12 November 2026 · Asia/Manila**  
**Scope** 25 cases. Explicit source numbers are marked. Other values are hypothetical. M06 checks arithmetic, evidence admissibility and counterexamples. There is no order, simulated strategy run, or market outcome in these cases.  
**References** [M06 specification](../methodology/09-m06-risk-and-execution-uncertainty.md) · [Public product receipts](phase2-m06-binance-public-metadata.md) · [Original Coach PDF](../../sources/coach%20trading%20lessons.pdf)

Unless stated otherwise, the abstract product is linear, $m=1$, with price in quote per base unit, quantity in base units, account currency equal to quote, and entry, stop and target as references. Zero cost applies only to cases explicitly framed as gross arithmetic. “Qualified” below refers to the described research gate. Public filter examples are conditional feasibility checks on stipulated prices and orders.

## Verification boundary

Manually checked signed geometry, per-target monotonicity and endpoint selection, dimensions, cost and funding signs, ratio arithmetic, time-order counterexamples, quantity conservation and terminal behavior. Exact decimal and rational arithmetic cross-checked the numeric examples. Product filter fields and source wording were verified separately. Paper expectations provide no outcome frequency, observed fills, portfolio loss bound or executed tests.
