## Terminal identity and reachability

At a fixed width, every admitted pivot origin advances with its confirmation bar. Appending and shifting keep the latest origin per side monotone. Same-side resets, a dual extreme or invalid history can clear the chain, but a subsequent confirmation cannot introduce an earlier origin. The only role selected per side comes from its most recent retained swing. A reference can be absent temporarily or change role at the same origin. After a newer origin on that side has been selected and becomes terminal, the older one cannot be selected again under these rules. The v0.1.4 helper-only demonstration injects an origin that bypasses this invariant.

**VERIFIED for the modeled fixed-width engine:** 19,683 exhaustive nine-bar paths over three prices at width 1; 784 width/policy runs over 96 longer constructed paths, the frozen receipt and one gapped copy. The longer runs covered 1,328 candidate events. No per-side origin regression or duplicate terminal event occurred. A separate 2,304-bar stress replay passed. Width changes create a new full Pine calculation with its own state, so an event can differ between configurations. This does not establish what happens after provider history revisions.

**INDEPENDENT_EXTENSION / EXPERIMENTAL:** one terminal-origin watermark per side. Equal origin retains terminal status, an older presented origin reports RETIRED, and a strictly newer origin follows normal rules even at the same price. No terminal history array or arbitrary eviction. The safeguard leaves the fixed-width reachable path unaffected and defends the helper if its callers evolve. R1-B retains its separate RESET PENDING path.
