## Rule-gap resolution algorithm (applies to EVERY module)

For each missing rule: **(1)** cite the original PDF page/figure and relevant PCSS line/lesson; **(2)** state precisely which required detail Coach left unquantified; **(3)** propose 2 materially different AI-defined policies or a defensible abstention baseline; **(4)** choose the simplest causal, falsifiable research default provisionally with a named rule ID/version; **(5)** pin counterexamples showing alternative outcomes and verify an independent test oracle; **(6)** keep a status/provenance label, configurable parameter, and uncertainty where data is insufficient; **(7)** challenge with verified closed-bar market data and revise by versioned evidence, not by aesthetic appeal or cherry-picked wins. No indefinite veto just because human confirmation is unavailable.

A sample baseline, **not source-certified**: S1 strict pivots left/right 2/2 and R1-A latest-close arm as first experimental comparison; R1-B re-cross as testable challenger; zero tick buffer initially, compare tick-buffer sensitivity. The exact size, role assignment, timeframe hierarchy, ties and signal classification are independently proposed. An indicator should call these `experimental structural candidate`, never `Coach-confirmed BOS`.

**AI review:** Distinct models or independent verification algorithms may check correctness. Multiple prompts to the same model, shared fixtures or self-reported agreement are not independent human observations. Treat disagreements as evidence about the models, not as votes that prove the Coach's intent.
