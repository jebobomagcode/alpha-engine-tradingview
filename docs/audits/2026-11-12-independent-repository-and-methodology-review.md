## A. Executive assessment

Alpha Engine TradingView is a source-grounded specification project for an eventual crypto research indicator. Phase 1 has a complete 26-unit study ledger. Phase 2 contains substantive candidate specifications for structural events, price zones, reaction and confirmation, and multi-timeframe decisions. The repository contains 140 written Phase 2 scenario rows. The current tree and workflow records provide no evidence of executable fixtures, Pine behavior, market outcomes, or deployment.

Preserve the indicator-first direction and source-specific setup branches. The immediate dependency is a **cross-module causal reconciliation**. Four concrete results motivate it:

1. R2's TIME-14 assumes a valid candle traded above its own final high. That fixture is impossible under ordinary, unrevised OHLC.
2. The validation protocol's no-backdating wording uses one time for two legitimate coordinates. A pivot can belong to an earlier bar while its confirmation becomes observable later. The historical drawing and the first actionable output need distinct assertions.
3. R2-A's later-bar rule permits a reaction bar to open before delayed touch evidence becomes available. The contract needs an explicit choice between strict sequential evidence and a decision-time classification that learns both observations together.
4. R1-A tests the last available close at arming, which can miss a pre-arm breach that reversed before arming. A latest-close policy is defensible, but differs from an ever-crossed prohibition.

These results concern specifications, source traceability, or logical counterexamples. They do not estimate market edge. The recommended next mission is to settle these boundary contracts and adjudicate one continuation profile on paper before M06 consumes its outputs. ### How the research design fits together

1. **Evidence (M00).** Identify the venue, instrument, product, candle calendar, price basis and data version. Qualify OHLC, finality, history and the earliest time each input is observable.
2. **Observations (M01–M04).** A swing is a reference candidate with an origin and later confirmation. A BOS needs a continuation reference under a prior regime. A CHoCH is a possible change using an eligible opposing reference. S/R, OB, FVG and Fib produce candidate location intervals with lifecycle and provenance. Candle geometry describes a reaction at that location. EMA and RSI describe price-derived context.
3. **Scenario (M05).** The selected style specifies which observations are required and when. Trend continuation, range, countertrend scalp, pullback scalp and reversal/breakout watch have distinct conditions. A new structural event changes the interpretation of the affected timeframe. Propagation to another timeframe requires its own qualifying evidence.
4. **Risk and evaluation (M06–M07).** Review the geometry and completeness of the proposal after its trigger conditions are established. Any execution study would require a separately specified order and cost model. Source fidelity, logical correctness, runtime behavior and market performance are distinct questions.

A qualified, pre-known zone without contact yields WAIT_ZONE. A qualifying completed contact without reaction yields WAIT_CONFIRMATION. Missing required data, structure or zone support yields the corresponding INSUFFICIENT status. A developing touch can be provisional. INVALIDATED means an identified scenario or zone failed a declared rule. REASSESS records a changed premise that requires a fresh evaluation. Keep opposing paths visible with their individual evidence and scope. Risk-incomplete can coexist with a valid descriptive reaction.

The research can already express OHLC geometry on valid input, interval overlap, strict close comparison against a frozen reference, selected Fibonacci coordinates and nominal stop/target distances. End-to-end classification remains underdetermined by swing selection, regime handover, activation and episode boundaries, confirmation timing, and profile dependencies. Candidate validity is conditional on the chosen specifications. ## F. Ponder findings

### Causal decisions and source-faithful decisions are different checks

T0 and T1 can both be causal. A final 4H bar received at 12:00:03 cannot support a 12:00:02 decision or an 11:55 opening context. It can support a *new* 12:00:04 assessment under T1. A stable confirmed Pine series can provide a reproducible chart-bar boundary, while exchange/provider reception timestamps require additional evidence. Choose the clock the indicator can observe before asking runtime to reproduce a stronger clock contract. Keep nominal end, native available time where measured, and evaluation boundary distinguishable. **INDEPENDENT_EXTENSION / OPEN_UNRESOLVED for runtime.** [M05 timing contract](../methodology/07-mtf-context-conflicts-and-causal-states.md#c-the-causal-what-did-we-know-contract) · [Pine other timeframes and data](https://www.tradingview.com/pine-script-docs/concepts/other-timeframes-and-data/) · [Pine execution model](https://www.tradingview.com/pine-script-docs/language/execution-model/)

### Three kinds of confluence deserve different treatment

**VALIDATED, formal.** For an unrounded same-source close EMA with fixed $0<\alpha<1$, the increments $E_t-E_{t-1}$ and $C_t-E_t$ are positive multiples of $C_t-E_{t-1}$. Their signs give one fact. A multi-bar slope can add a different descriptive fact. For example, with $\alpha=1/2$, $E_{t-2}=110$, $C_{t-1}=90$, and $C_t=104$, the EMA moves from 100 to 102 while its two-bar slope remains negative. The same-bar close is above 102. For Wilder RSI with matching linear smoothing and initialization on nonnegative up/down changes $G,D$, $\mathrm{RSI}-50=50(G-D)/(G+D)$ when $G+D>0$. Linearity gives $G-D$ as the Wilder-smoothed signed close change and $G+D$ as the smoothed absolute close change. This explains why RSI midpoint and the sign of that specific smoothed return give one fact. At $G=D=0$, the formula is undefined and a platform convention is required. This algebra does not equate RSI and EMA to each other. [EMA research](../research/18-ema-slope-redundancy-range-candidates.md) · [RSI research](../research/17-rsi-state-momentum-candidates.md)

**INDEPENDENT_EXTENSION.** Handle exact duplicates by identity, shared-bar dependencies by provenance, and incremental predictive usefulness by comparison. Two detectors can use the same bars and still capture different information. Conversely, the p14 OB candle and its linked gap, or overlapping labels from the same impulse, can be shown together without counting them as statistically independent. A three-candle geometric FVG only excludes overlap of the first and third ranges under the chosen formula. The middle candle can have traded inside the interval. It does not establish market depth, lack of transactions, or participant identity. [Zones](../methodology/03-zones-sr-ob-fvg-fibonacci.md) · [Confluence ablation research](../research/15-indicator-reliability-confluence-ablation.md)

### Waiting is an observable output

A useful research indicator preserves the reason a scenario has not qualified, what observation would change that reason, and how old the evidence is. This makes a conservative detector testable: a variant can improve apparent agreement by abstaining on difficult cases. Report coverage and detection delay with disagreement, including missing-data coverage and ambiguity coverage. Compare stricter variants on the same opportunities before judging the tradeoff. **INDEPENDENT_EXTENSION / EXPERIMENTAL.** [M05 statuses](../methodology/07-mtf-context-conflicts-and-causal-states.md#f-proposed-status-vocabulary-provenance-and-deterministic-explanation) · [M07 protocol](../research/phase2-mtf-validation-and-falsification-protocol.md)

A future technical pass must distinguish price distance from cash and margin risk. For a simple linear instrument the nominal loss at stop is $q m \lvert E-S\rvert$, before costs and gap exposure, with quantity $q$ and payout multiplier $m$. As a narrow mathematical check, if a full win pays gross $g$, a full loss costs $d$, and their costs are $c_W,c_L$, the binary break-even probability is $\frac{d+c_L}{g+d+c_L-c_W}$ when net wins are positive and losses are strictly positive. For the illustrative 2R/1R case with costs 0.1R on either outcome, it is $1.1/3\approx36.67\%$, versus $1/3$ with no costs. This model omits variable fills, partial exits and serial dependence. A single source RR or an emulator's fill assumption cannot validate net expectancy.
